# Joining Friends SMP

About 5 minutes, once. After that you just click **Launch**, and new or updated mods download
by themselves.

**You need:** Minecraft: Java Edition on your Microsoft account (Windows or Mac), and Samuel
needs to add your Minecraft username to the server's whitelist. Send it to him first!

## 1. Install Prism Launcher

Download it from **https://prismlauncher.org/download** and install it. It's free and open source,
and it's the launcher we use instead of the official one.

When it opens for the first time, a short setup wizard appears. The defaults are fine. If it asks
about Java, let Prism **download Java automatically**.

## 2. Sign in

Click **Accounts** (top right) → **Manage Accounts** → **Add Microsoft**, and sign in with the
account that owns Minecraft.

## 3. Add our modpack

1. Click **Add Instance** (top left).
2. Choose **Import** on the left side.
3. Paste this link and click **OK**:

   ```
   https://samueldpackard.github.io/friends-smp/Friends-SMP.zip
   ```

A **Friends-SMP** instance appears in Prism's main window.

## 4. Play

Click **Friends-SMP** once, then click **Launch** on the right. The first time, a small window downloads the mods
(about a minute). Then Minecraft starts.

In Minecraft: **Multiplayer → Add Server**, Server Address: **`35.160.13.72`**.

**If the server is asleep** (the list says "asleep - join to wake it up"), just click Join. You'll
see "Waking up the server!". Wait about a minute and join again. The server goes back to sleep
by itself 15 minutes after the last person leaves, so we only pay while someone is playing.

That's it. Always launch from Prism, from the **Friends-SMP** instance. Each launch checks for
mod updates, so you're always in sync with the server.

## Extras that are already included

- **Inventory sorting** (Inventory Profiles Next): press **R** to sort a chest or your inventory.
  Its settings are under Mod Menu (Mods button on the title screen).
- **Food info** (AppleSkin) and **what am I looking at** (Jade) tooltips.
- **Classic music:** everyday music is C418's original soundtrack only. Caves, the Deep Dark,
  Cherry Groves and the Nether keep their own special music.
- **Lava sponge** (Samuel's own mod): craft a sponge surrounded by 4 lava buckets (top, left,
  right, bottom). Place it and it soaks up all lava within 7 blocks, and any lava that later
  flows into it, then glows like a lamp. Dry a wet one by crafting it with packed ice.
- **Sleds** (Samuel's own mod): craft one from any wood type: sticks in the top corners, 3
  planks across the middle, iron ingots in the bottom corners. Place it on the ground and
  right-click to ride (2 seats). It glides on snow and ice (faster on ice) and crawls on
  anything else; W kicks off, S brakes, A/D steer, and hills speed you up. Riders never sink
  into powder snow.
  - **Chest:** right-click the sled with a chest; sneak + right-click to open it.
  - **Carpet:** right-click with any carpet for a coloured seat that keeps riders from freezing.
    Shears take it off.
  - **Dog sled:** put a lead on your tame wolf, then sneak + right-click the sled. The wolf
    jumps into the harness in front. Ride and hold W and it pulls you along, uphill too; let go
    to coast. Shears on the lead (or making the wolf sit) unhitch it.
  - Break the sled (punch it) to pick it back up with everything on it.
- **Holiday food** (Samuel's own mod, Holiday Feast): gingerbread cookies, hot cocoa, eggnog,
  peppermint candy canes, fruitcake, mince pie, peppermint bark and roast ham. The recipe book
  shows each one once you pick up an ingredient. Mint Leaves sometimes drop when you break grass;
  plant them on farmland.
- **Snowball fights** (Snowballing):
  - **Packed Snowball Piles:** right-click a single layer of snow with a shovel to make a pile, and
    keep shoveling to add snowballs (up to 6). Right-click a snowball with an empty hand to take
    that one as a **Packed Snowball**. Sneak + right-click with one to put it back.
  - **Villager duels:** hit a villager with a Packed Snowball (regular snowballs don't count) and it
    gathers snowballs and fights back. Your duels show on the left of the screen with the score.
    Do well and the villager throws you emeralds (more the bigger your win); then it won't duel
    you again for 5 minutes. Villagers only fight where there's snow to make snowballs from.
    Friends can join in on the same villager: everyone has their own duel with it, with their own
    score and gift. It throws at whoever is nearest, and throws faster when outnumbered.
  - Throws have a short cooldown (the attack meter under your crosshair).
  - **Snow Sling** (a rare duel prize): right-click a pile with it to load it, then draw it like a
    bow. A full draw flies as far as an arrow and counts as 3 hits.
- **Holiday decorations:** Macaw's Holidays and DecorationX: Christmas add hundreds of Christmas
  blocks (trees, lights, presents, fireplaces, snowmen) in the creative menu and recipe book.
  DecorationX's snowmen can also come to life: see their tooltips.
- **Shaders (off unless you turn them on):** Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs → pick
  **Complementary Reimagined** (prettier lighting, shadows, water and swaying plants; it still
  looks like Minecraft). Too slow? Its settings have presets from Potato to Ultra. Only one
  shader pack can be on at a time.
- **Connected glass (off unless you turn it on):** Options → Resource Packs → move **Clean
  Connected Glass** to the right, then Done. Glass placed side by side looks like one big pane.

## Problems?

- **"Pre-launch command failed"**: usually no internet connection. Check it and launch again.
- **Can't connect: "mod mismatch" or "incompatible"**: close Minecraft and launch again from
  Prism. That re-syncs the mods.
- **Something else:** send Samuel a screenshot.
